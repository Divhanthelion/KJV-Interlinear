# Privacy Policy

**KJV Interlinear** collects no personal data.

- The app works entirely offline. The Bible text, Hebrew and Greek data, and lexicons are built into the app.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and it makes no network requests of its own.
- Your settings, bookmarks, and reading history are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- Links in the About section (eBible.org, STEP Bible, GitHub) open in your web browser only when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
