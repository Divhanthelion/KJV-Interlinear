## Pronunciations

- Entries marked "CMU dictionary" in `lexicon/kjv.tsv` use Arpabet
  transcriptions from the CMU Pronouncing Dictionary, Copyright (C) 1993-2015
  Carnegie Mellon University, redistributed under its BSD-style licence
  (http://www.speech.cs.cmu.edu/cgi-bin/cmudict). The licence asks that the
  copyright notice and conditions be kept with redistributions; the full text
  is in the dictionary download.
- Entries marked "ISBE (1915)" are respellings from The International Standard
  Bible Encyclopedia, ed. James Orr, 1915, public domain.
- "Random House" notes cite that dictionary for the respelling only; no text of
  it is reproduced.
